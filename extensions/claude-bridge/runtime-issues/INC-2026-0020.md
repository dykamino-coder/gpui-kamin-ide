## Symptom

After reported Windows sleep or an RDP disconnect lasting about 5–10 minutes,
a Windows stopped-working dialog appears. Closing it restored the Bridge and
Console panels without a full IDE restart. Supplied logs confirm CEF termination
and panel reinitialization, with a roughly three-minute gap in recorded samples.

  2026-09-07 07:56:54.346 and 07:56:55.822 UTC, both crashed with
  `-2147483645` / `0x80000003`, Microsoft's

- `crates/crash/src/lib.rs` records the observing main PID; the callback does

  processes. `crates/shell/src/web/browsers.rs:137–157` logs termination and
  reloads the affected frame's URL. Continued diag reports without a new boot
  marker agree with the owner's report that the shell survived.
- The four newly supplied extension incident generations reconstruct 12,058
  complete, chronological records: 11,224 samples and 834 transitions. The
  logger is extension-host throughout. Earlier startup-only host incident.log
  is a separate file; its single September 4 startup remains expected.
- Last pre-callback samples were chat 07:53:50.620 and tools 07:53:50.698 UTC.
  Next samples were 07:56:54.854 and 07:56:56.394: gaps of 184.234/185.696 s,
  the only gaps over 30 s for either role in the retained period. New samples
  arrived 508/572 ms after the callbacks. Chat first had an empty store, then
  retained entries again, consistent with the immediate-on-mount sample in

- Recorded samples in the prior 30 minutes show chat JS heap 23–24 MiB and tools

- A post-callback transition moves from connecting at 07:57:24.746 to connected
  at 07:57:25.003. This shows one recorded session connection recovery, not all
  panels/sessions or the remote server's complete state.

## Interpretation and counterchecks

The sample gap is an observation gap, not a proven sleep/freeze/crash duration.
Extension diagnostics stamp receipt time and logger PID; timer suspension,
Windows sleep, transport delay or an observer pause can interrupt this trail.
The tools role does not independently identify Console or a renderer process.
Recorded recovery does not prove every subscription/pending-input contract survived.

Three actual record splits confirm INC-2026-0026, but none overlaps the sample
gap. The field's 327 disconnected/unknown records cannot establish 327 network
outages: INC-2026-0027 independently confirms close-code loss before classification,
while explicit local disconnects can also produce unknown. Earlier untimed host
RPC rejections are likewise not a reliable crash-time remote-server trace.

The main-process GPUI hypothesis does not explain the surviving main and panel
reload; independently confirmed native hazards remain in INC-2026-0021–0025.
Relevant callback/diagnostic source and bundled extension are unchanged between
the a34ab48 audit and current main `365a56ade9915811b2034cc205ac7eea11ae466e`.
The documented installer-source comparison remains in private evidence; a version
label does not authenticate the installed executable or libcef DLL hashes.

## Expected outcome

Obtain an existing Windows Application Error/WER event or dump near the callbacks:
actual victim PID/image, faulting module/address/stack or bounded CEF fatal message.
Correlate existing power/display/RDP events around 07:53–07:57 UTC to distinguish
sleep from connection-only changes. The extension globalStorage trail has now
been supplied; another full IDE restart is not required to read retained files.
Preserve raw logs/dumps privately and retain the observer/victim distinction.

After attribution, use a disposable Windows gate for sleep and 5–10 minute RDP
disconnect, fresh/long-running sessions and relevant rendering modes. Check shell
survival, panel load, Bridge handshake/subscriptions, Console state, pending input,
reload loops and resource growth. No product fix or native reproduction is
included; keep the incident open until attribution and applicable acceptance.
