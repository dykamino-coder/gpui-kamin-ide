## Confirmed behavior

An MCP server can write diagnostic progress on stderr and still respond normally to JSON-RPC initialize and tools/list. The stdio transport counted every stderr line against its repeat and burst limits and logged routine progress through console.error. A synthetic process writing repeated INFO lines before its handshake was killed by the circuit breaker.

## Acceptance

Start the synthetic stdio peer, read its tools after a burst of INFO lines, and check that a child repeatedly reporting a failed connection still trips the breaker. Use the existing UTF-8 transport test as a control.
