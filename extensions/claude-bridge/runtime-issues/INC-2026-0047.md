## Symptom

After `/rewind`, Console follows the selected conversation correctly but Chat
keeps the old or otherwise incorrect history. Sending another message makes
the divergence visible; Chat does not converge to the active conversation.
Which rewind option was selected (conversation, files, or both) is unknown.
