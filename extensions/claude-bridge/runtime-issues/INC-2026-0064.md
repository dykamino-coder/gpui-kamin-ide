## Symptom

Any change on screen — a blinking caret, a spinner, a hover state — makes
KaminIDE repaint the entire window. With a GPU this is cheap; on the software
rasterizer (WARP: RDP sessions, VMs without a GPU) every frame costs CPU in
proportion to the whole window area (INC-2026-0001 measured ~12 Mpx painted per
frame for a 1.8 Mpx window before #261).

## Confirmed facts (main `b1ff5c2`, 2026-10-15)

- GPUI caches unchanged views on the CPU side (`reuse_prepaint`/`reuse_paint`
  in `vendor/gpui/src/window.rs`): unchanged views are not rebuilt.
- The scene keeps a `BoundsTree` for primitive ordering and hit testing, but
  there is no damage region: all primitives of the scene are submitted to the
  GPU every frame.
- The DirectX renderer (`vendor/gpui-windows/src/directx_renderer.rs`) clears
  and redraws the full frame: `ScissorEnable: false`, no `Present1` with dirty
  rectangles, and the swapchain presents the whole back buffer.
- The built-in HTML engine (`crates/html/src/render/mod.rs`, `render(nodes,
  opts)`) rebuilds the cascade, layout and GPUI elements of the whole page for
  any change; there is no style or layout invalidation by subtree. This is
  tracked here as context; the engine pipeline is a separate follow-up.

## Expected direction (to be confirmed by the design)

1. Compute a damage region per frame from what changed between scenes
   (views that were rebuilt, moved or re-composited; CEF texture updates).
2. Redraw only primitives intersecting the damage, with a scissor per damage
   rectangle, preserving the rest of the previous frame (a swapchain mode that
   keeps the back buffer contents, or an explicit copy).
3. Present with dirty rectangles (`IDXGISwapChain1::Present1`).
4. Fall back to a full repaint on resize, device loss and DPI change.
