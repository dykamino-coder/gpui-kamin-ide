## Symptom and available evidence

The owner reports excessive scrolling through blank space after the last Projects group. A September 7 screenshot shows mixed expanded/collapsed groups, inactive toggles, an empty project and a large black region below the final visible group, together with a scrollbar.

## Limits and deduplication

The screenshot does not by itself establish the total scroll extent or prove that ordinary unused viewport space is scrollable. No dynamic offset/bounds trace or exact trigger is supplied. This tail is distinct from INC-2026-0029's inter-group gap and INC-2026-0032's hover/scroll transition; investigate overlap without presuming a common cause. BR-28's bounded no-reflow result remains unchanged.

## Maintainer research deliverable

Inspect native GPUI Projects list construction in crates/shell/src/ui/sessions_list.rs and ui/sessions, and identify its actual scroll/layout owner. Capture viewport height, content extent, current/max offset and last row/group bounds using native metrics; DOM scrollHeight is not an assumed API for this panel.

Compare expanded/collapsed groups, inactive rows, No sessions yet, hidden/removed rows and selected sessions above/inside/below the viewport. Measure before and after expand/collapse, add/delete/disconnect, active/inactive changes, hover and tab switching. Inspect spacers or cached height only if that mechanism actually exists.

## Acceptance and next handoff

Provide a paired reproduction identifying the first excess extent owner, or a measured explanation that the visible blank area is ordinary viewport space. A later fix must make scroll range match content while preserving scroll position, edge actions and selection visibility, with geometry regression coverage and a Windows GPUI gate.
