## Symptom

Typing `/compact` in a GPT (Codex) chat sends the text `/compact` to the model, which answers it as a message. Bridge has no manual compaction for Codex chats.

## Confirmed facts

- Automatic compaction of Codex threads works (forced in the #1047 gate: the thread compacted and kept its memory).
- Manual `/compact` is handled for Claude chats only.
