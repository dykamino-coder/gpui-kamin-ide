## Symptom

After one engine answers, switch to a second engine and return without submitting a turn to the second engine. The server-generated handoff can claim that the second engine answered.

## Confirmed facts

- `planSwitch` passes the outgoing engine and model to the handoff formatter, including when that segment has no turns.
- `fromDigest` describes that engine as the author of earlier answers without checking whether the retained assistant turns came from it.
- The actual session WebSocket path reproduced this combination with synthetic engines on the 1.0.129 source baseline.

## Expected outcome

The joining engine receives the earlier user and assistant turns. The handoff may describe the engine selection and must not attribute answers to a segment based on that selection alone. Exercise the empty intermediate segment and a switch after an answered turn in both directions.

## Missing evidence and blockers

The historical field cause does not affect the independently reproducible server contract. Confirmed source behavior and delivery are tracked separately.
